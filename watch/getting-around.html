Getting around your home screen

The swipes that take you between your home screen, Everything else, your notifications and Settings.

The steps, in words
  1. Swipe from right to left for Everything else. The pages follow your finger.
  2. Swipe back to your home screen.
  3. Swipe up to find an app. Everything else opens with its search, ready to type.
  4. Back sends it down again.
  5. Swipe down for your notifications. Swipe up to put them away.
  6. Press and hold an empty part of the home screen. Settings opens.

A few swipes, and you're wherever you need to be.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
