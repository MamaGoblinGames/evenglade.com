Taking a break from Evenglade

Step away for a while with your phone's own home screen, and come back to everything just as you left it.

The steps, in words
  1. To take a break, open Settings and scroll to Advanced. To open Settings, press and hold an empty part of the home screen.
  2. Tap Stop using Evenglade. Every app opens and notifies as usual, and your choices stay as they are.
  3. It asks for your key. If someone you trust holds it, ask them for a code.
  4. Tap Stop using it.
  5. Your phone's own home screen is back. Evenglade rests until you open it again.
  6. To start again, open Evenglade from your apps.
  7. Tap Start using Evenglade again. The apps you've set aside rest again.
  8. Make Evenglade your home screen once more: choose Evenglade, then Set as default.
  9. Evenglade is back, just as you left it, and your set-aside apps are resting again.

Take a break whenever you need one. Evenglade waits just as you left it.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
