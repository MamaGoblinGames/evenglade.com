Your apps

Where each of your apps lives, and how to move one, move several at once, or set one aside.

The steps, in words
  1. Your apps shows where each app lives. Open it from Everything else, or from Settings.
  2. Each place says what it's for: the few apps you reach for most on your home screen, the rest in Everything else.
  3. Unlisted apps are off your lists, but still at work. They open from notifications, links and other apps, like Android Auto.
  4. Set-aside apps rest, out of sight and quiet. Bringing one back asks for your key.
  5. Tap an app to move it. Moving an app further away never needs your key.
  6. To move several at once, tap Move several apps at once. Check them in their places, then tap Move and choose where.
  7. Tap Set it aside to put an app out of sight. Bringing it back will ask for your key.
  8. The app is set aside, and its notifications are cleared. It rests under Set aside until you bring it back.

Every app has a place, and you choose it.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
