Getting started

The walkthrough Evenglade begins with, from your reasons to look up to your new home screen, holding the key yourself.

The steps, in words
  1. Evenglade starts with a short walkthrough. Each step has a short video to watch. Tap Begin when you're ready.
  2. First, a few reasons to look up from your phone. Evenglade brings one up now and then.
  3. Choose your look. You can change it later.
  4. Then, where your apps live: Home screen, Everything else, Set aside and Unlisted, in a few words each.
  5. Choose up to five apps for your home screen. The rest wait in Everything else, a swipe away.
  6. Some apps start out set aside. Tap any you'd like to keep. It's free to change now, before you choose your key.
  7. Choose a daily aim, if you'd like one.
  8. Then choose who'll hold your key. Someone you trust can choose the PIN instead: that path has its own guide.
  9. Holding it yourself, you choose a PIN. Type it, tap Set PIN, then type it once more.
  10. You're all set, with your choices in one place. Tap Go home.

Change any of these whenever you like, in Settings.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
