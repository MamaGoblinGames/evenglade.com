Press and hold to move apps

Move an app right where you see it, on your home screen or in Everything else.

The steps, in words
  1. Press and hold an app on your home screen. Its menu lets you move it, or open App info.
  2. App info opens Android's own page for the app.
  3. It works the same in Everything else.
  4. Tap Move to your home screen, and the app is close at hand. Swipe back to your home screen to see it.
  5. Changed your mind? Press and hold to move it back.
  6. Set it aside puts an app out of sight, and its notifications are cleared. Bringing it back will ask for your key.

Press and hold any app to choose where it lives.

When you're done, swipe back to return to Evenglade.
