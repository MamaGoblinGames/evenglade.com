Releases

What's new in each version of Evenglade. Testers on Google Play get each one as it comes out.

0.1.23

Coming soon

A clearer start, and a clearer picture of where your apps live and how long you'd like to spend in each.

  • A walkthrough that explains more. A new step shows where your apps live. You choose up to five apps for your home screen to start, and decide what's set aside while it's still free to change, before choosing your key. Each step links to a short video, and the last one gathers your choices in one place.
  • Your aims. A page of its own that groups your apps by their aims, from 0m to 2h a day. Set an aim for any app by pressing and holding it, too.
  • Your apps says what each place is for, and Move several apps at once lets you check the apps right where they are.
  • Android Auto starts out unlisted, so it doesn't get in the way of connecting to your car.
  • Your look's wallpaper comes back when you return to Evenglade after a break.
  • What the words mean, a short page in Settings, under Feedback and help.
  • The screen time lines in Settings say when they're waiting for Usage access.
