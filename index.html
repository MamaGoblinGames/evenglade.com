Evenglade // A calmer phone

A calmer phone, with space for your life.

Evenglade is a home screen for Android that keeps the few apps you need close and sets the rest aside until you choose to bring them back. No account, no ads, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

Set aside, on your terms

The apps that pull you out of the now, resting until you choose them.

Your home screen holds only the few apps that matter. Everything else waits a tap away, and the apps you'd rather not reach for out of habit are set aside. If one is opened some other way, from a link or a search, a calm Evenglade screen covers it. Turn on notification access, and their notifications are cleared as they arrive.

Your apps, near and far

The few apps you need sit up front. Everything else is a tap away, and the apps that pull you away rest out of sight. Any you'd rather not see at all can be unlisted, and still open from a link.

Moving an app further away never needs your key. Bringing one back from being set aside does.

A breath before you open, and your reason to look up

Name what you're making time for, such as a walk with the dog, a good book or playing music, and Evenglade brings it up now and then on your home screen. Set gentle aims for your time, for the day or for one app. Once an aim has passed, opening that app begins with a short, breathing pause and one of your reasons.

Aims are gentle boundaries, not lockouts. They never block an app, only remind you of your intentions.

Screen time, quietly

If you'd like to see it, today's screen time and unlocks sit at the top of your home screen, and each app's time beside its name, next to any aim you've set.

It's counted from Android's own record, on your phone, and never leaves it.

Your key, and someone you trust if you'd like

  • Bring an app back for 15 minutes, half an hour or an hour, with your PIN. It's set aside again on time, with a countdown in your notifications.
  • If you'd like help, a trusted friend can hold the key for you, with one-time codes.
  • They never see what you do on your phone, and they don't need an account either.

Your look

A home screen that feels like yours.

Ten looks, from a numbered list on sage green to pencil on a pocket memo pad. Evenglade and the Clean looks can also follow your phone's light and dark mode.

What stays

It's still your phone.

Evenglade is an app on Android, not a new operating system, so your phone keeps everything it already does well.

  • /Android's security updates
  • /Your number, your carrier, calls and texts
  • /Your camera and your photos
  • /Maps, and any app you choose to keep close
  • /No account to make, no ads, no tracking

Trying it early

Evenglade is in testing on Google Play.

A small group is trying it before it opens to everyone, and you're welcome to join. All you need is an Android phone and the Google account you use on it.

  1. Join the testers group. Join the Evenglade testers group with the Google account on your phone. If the accounts differ, Google Play won't know you're a tester.
  2. Become a tester. Open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Evenglade. Get it from Google Play. Updates arrive just like any other app's.
  4. Tell us how it's going. On Evenglade's page in the Play Store, scroll to the section for testers and send your feedback there. It comes straight to us, and it's never shown publicly.

If you can, keep Evenglade for two weeks or more. Google asks for a steady group of testers over 14 days before an app can open to everyone, so each day you keep it helps.

Will the group fill my inbox?
No. Only we post there, and only when there's something new to try. You can also turn its emails off in Google Groups.
Which phones does it work on?
Android 10 or newer. It's built and tested on Pixels, and it's likely to work on other Android phones too. If you try it on one, we'd love to hear how it goes.
Is this a launcher?
It's a home screen, with a little more: when a set-aside app is opened some other way, Evenglade covers it, and it can quiet that app's notifications.
Does it use the internet?
Never. Evenglade has no internet access at all, so your choices, reasons and screen time stay on your phone.
What if I change my mind?
Choose another home app in Android's Settings, under Default apps, or uninstall Evenglade. Every app is right where it was.
