Evenglade // A calmer phone

Privacy

Effective November 7, 2026

In short

Evenglade collects no data about you. It has no internet access at all: Android doesn't let it reach the internet, because it never asks to. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything Evenglade knows stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Evenglade keeps your choices so it can work: which apps are on your home screen, unlisted or set aside; your look; what you're making time for; your aims; which apps are back for now; and, with notification access on, how many notifications it quieted from each set-aside app over the past week. It also keeps your key:

  • The PIN you choose for the app is stored only as a salted, one-way hash, mixed with a key held in your phone's secure hardware, so the PIN itself is never kept and a copy of Evenglade's files is no use on any other device.
  • One-time codes are optional. If you set them up, you scan a QR code from Evenglade with an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator. That QR code holds a secret key that both apps use to make the same six-digit codes. Evenglade keeps its copy of the key on your phone, locked with a key held in your phone's secure hardware, and never sends it anywhere. Your authenticator app keeps its own copy.

None of this is included in Android's backups or in transfers to a new phone. It describes this phone only. Uninstalling Evenglade removes all of it.

What Evenglade uses on your phone, and why

Each of these is used on the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere.

PermissionWhat it's used for
Usage accessReads Android's own record of app use, the one Digital Wellbeing uses, to show your screen time, keep track of your aims, and notice when a set-aside app comes to the front.
Accessibility service (optional)Notices only when a different app comes to the front, so a set-aside app can be covered right away. It can't read what's on the screen or what you type.
Notification access (optional)Checks which app each notification comes from, and clears it if that app is set aside. Also checks which app is playing music or video, and pauses it if that app is set aside. It never reads a notification's title or text, or what's playing. It keeps only a count of how many it cleared from each app over the past week, shown in Your apps, and older counts are deleted.
NotificationsEvenglade's own notes, such as the countdown while an app is back for a while.
WallpaperSets the wallpaper that matches your chosen look.
Run at startupStarts Evenglade's quiet work again after your phone restarts or Evenglade updates.
Change secure settings (only if allowed from a computer)Turns Android's grayscale on and off for you. Android grants it only from a computer, during setup.
Device management (optional)Only if you set Evenglade up to look after the phone, from a computer: Android's device management lets it put set-aside apps away, hold new installs, and keep your choices steady.

Sharing

Evenglade doesn't share, sell or transfer any data, because it doesn't collect any. A trusted person who keeps your key, or reads you a one-time code, never sees your apps, your screen time or anything else you do.

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and to add you to a test if you ask to join one. We don't share them.

This website

evenglade.com uses no cookies and no analytics. Its fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them, and like any website, the service that hosts it may keep standard logs of requests.

Children

Evenglade doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Evenglade ever needed to collect anything, it would ask first, and this page would say what and why.
